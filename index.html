Privacy Policy for EMI Atlas

Effective: 19 October 2026 · Android package: com.decordesigns.emiatlas
EMI Atlas performs loan calculations on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your loan amounts, income inputs, saved plans or reports. Google AdMob provides advertising and may process the data described below.

Who we are

EMI Atlas is provided by Decor Designs. Contact us at info@interiordecordesigns.in. Our website is interiordecordesigns.in.

Loan information on your device

The app uses amounts, interest rates, repayment terms, income and existing-payment inputs to calculate estimates. Current loan settings, payoff strategies, display preferences and plans you save are stored in the app's private storage. These inputs are not sent to Decor Designs or included in advertising requests. No account, bank connection, credit score or financial account access is required. The app does not request contacts, microphone, camera or precise-location permissions.

Advertising and consent

The app includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). These services may collect or share device and other identifiers (including the Advertising ID where available), IP address and approximate location derived from it, app and advertising interactions, diagnostic information, crash logs and performance information. Google uses this data for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security and service operation. SDK data is transmitted using encryption in transit.

We request non-personalized ads and restricted data processing for Google ad requests. Non-personalized advertising can still use device identifiers and other data for purposes such as frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. These settings do not mean no data is processed. Consent information is refreshed at launch; ads are requested only when UMP allows them. Where required, a Google consent form provides privacy choices. Available choices depend on your region. You can revisit required choices through the app's Settings screen.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google controls retention and processing of data received by its services. We do not receive a copy of the Advertising ID from the app or maintain a user-level advertising database.

PDF, CSV and sharing

When you export a report, the app creates a PDF or CSV in its local cache and opens Android's sharing controls. Files may contain loan values and the report title. You decide whether to send a report and which recipient or app receives it. A service you choose to share with will handle that file under its own policies. Copies outside the app are not removed when you delete a saved plan.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this to answer your request and troubleshoot. Please do not send bank credentials, identity documents or sensitive financial records. We retain support correspondence only as needed for support, security and applicable obligations; you can request deletion using the contact address above.

Storage, deletion and your choices

You can delete individual saved plans in the app. Clear the app's storage through Android settings or uninstall it to remove local plans, settings and app cache. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app. Separately saved or shared exports must be deleted from their destination. Android device settings also provide controls for your Advertising ID. Contact us with a privacy request; we cannot recover or delete local plans remotely because we do not have them.

Children

EMI Atlas is intended for adults aged 18 and over planning loans and repayments. It is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through our support service. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, contact us so we can address it.

Security and international processing

Local data uses Android's app storage protections. Keep your device secured and share reports carefully. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Google and services you choose may process information in countries other than yours under their own privacy terms and safeguards.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Any materially different data practices will be reflected in this policy and required in-app notices.
